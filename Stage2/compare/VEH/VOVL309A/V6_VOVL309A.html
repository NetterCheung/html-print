Industrial Tractor (Refuse Collection) Conditions of Licence
 
 
        The registration and licensing of an Industrial Tractor (Refuse Collection) (hereinafter referred as "IT (RC)") is subject to the following conditions which may be reviewed periodically:
 
  (1)
Driver driving an IT (RC) shall possess a valid full driving licence with driving entitlement "Special Purpose Vehicle" (vehicle class 21), and the Transport Department has approved the driver to drive that IT (RC);
 
  (2)
Operation of the IT (RC) and the associated implement is restricted to the route as previously approved by the Transport Department;
 
  (3)
The IT (RC) is only allowed to be used for refuse collection;
 
  (4)
Special attention must be paid at all times to the traffic nearby and the safety of pedestrians;
 
  (5)
The maximum design speed of the IT (RC) shall not exceed 10 km/hr on level ground;
 
  (6)
No undue obstruction should be caused to pedestrians and other vehicles;
 
  (7)
The vehicle registration mark shall be displayed at the front and rear of the IT (RC), vehicle registration mark shall also be displayed on the back of the rear most implement for refuse collection;
 
  (8)
Vehicle owner needs to acquire sufficient and appropriate amount of third party risks insurance for the equipment installed on the IT (RC) and operation of such equipment;
 
  (9)
The owner of the IT (RC) shall ensure that :-
 
    (i)
A flashing amber light conforming to the requirements laid down in regulation 111 of the Road Traffic (Construction and Maintenance of Vehicles) Regulations, Cap. 374A, is displayed on top of the IT (RC) and the rear most implement at all times when in use on the road;

    (ii)
The IT (RC) is fitted with an automatic device capable of giving audible and sufficient warning when it is reversing and is about to reverse and such device shall conform to the requirements laid down in regulation 38 of the Road Traffic (Construction and Maintenance of Vehicles) Regulations, Cap. 374A as applied to goods vehicle;
    (iii)
The IT (RC) is equipped with an efficient braking system including service and parking brake, steering system, adequate lighting system and other accessories deemed necessary for its safe operation;
 
    (iv)
The IT (RC) is properly maintained in good and safe working conditions for operation at all times;
 
  (10)
Vehicle licence must be shown on the vehicle, and driver of the IT (RC) must bring along the original of this document when driving, and produce it to police officer for inspection as and when required;
 
  (11)
Carriage of passengers is not allowed;
 
  (12)
The overall length of the IT (RC) with implement shall not exceed 8 meters, and the combined vehicle weight shall not exceed 5.5 tonnes;
 
  (13)
The IT (RC) shall be driven by electrical power;
 
  (14)
The use of the tractor to push the implement backward is not allowed. The implement could only be moved backward manually.
 
   
Note: IT (RC) and the associated implement are designed and used for refuse collection, according to regulation 2 of the Road Traffic (Construction and Maintenance of Vehicles) Regulations.

工業用拖拉機(廢物收集)的發牌條件
 
 
       工業用拖拉機(廢物收集)的登記及發牌須遵照以下條件，而有關條件可予以定期檢討：
 
  (1)
工業用拖拉機(廢物收集)的司機必須擁有香港正式駕駛執照「特別用途車輛」的駕駛資格(車輛類別代號21)，並已獲運輸署書面批准該司機駕駛該輛工業用拖拉機(廢物收集)；
 
  (2)
工業用拖拉機(廢物收集)及其器具只限於已獲運輸署批准的路線上運作；
 
  (3)
工業用拖拉機(廢物收集)只容許用作收集廢物；
 
  (4)
在任何時間均須特別注意附近的交通情況及關顧行人安全；
 
  (5)
工業用拖拉機(廢物收集)在平地上行駛的最高設計車速不得超逾每小時10公里；
 
  (6)
不得對行人及其他車輛造成不必要的阻礙；
 
  (7)
須在車頭及車尾展示車輛登記號碼，並在最後一個用作收集廢物的器具尾部展示車輛登記號碼；
 
  (8)
車主必須為工業用拖拉機(廢物收集)上裝設的設備及其操作購買足夠及合適的第三者保險；
 
  (9)
車主需確保 :-
 
    (i)
當工業用拖拉機(廢物收集)在道路上使用時，須時刻在車頂及最後一個用作收集廢物的器具尾部閃亮琥珀色的燈，該燈須符合《道路交通（車輛構造及保養）規例》(第374A章）第111條所訂下的規定；
 
    (ii)
工業用拖拉機(廢物收集)必須裝配一個能夠在其倒車時及即將倒車時發出聲音並可給予充分警告的自動裝置，而有關裝置須符合《道路交通（車輛構造及保養）規例》(第374A章）第38條適用於貨車的規定；

    (iii)
車主須確保工業用拖頭(廢物收集)配備有效的行車制動系統、停泊制動系統、轉向系統、充足的照明系統、發聲警告系統、倒後鏡及安全操作所需的其他附件；
 
    (iv)
工業用拖拉機(廢物收集)必須維修妥善，並時刻保持良好和安全操作狀況；
 
  (10)
必須在車輛上展示車輛牌照，而司機在駕駛該車輛時需攜帶本文件(正本)，以便在有需要時供警務人員查閱；
 
  (11)
不得接載乘客；
 
  (12)
工業用拖拉機(廢物收集)及其器具的總長度不可超過8米，而重量不可超過5.5公噸；
 
  (13)
工業用拖拉機(廢物收集)須以電力推動；
 
  (14)
工業用拖拉機(廢物收集)的器具只可用人力將其向後移動，不可用拖頭向後推動器具。
 
   
註: 根據《道路交通(車輛構造及保養)規例》第2條，工業用拖拉機(廢物收集)及其器具是為收集廢物而設計及使用的。
