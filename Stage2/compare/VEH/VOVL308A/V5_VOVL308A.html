Forklift Truck Conditions of Licence
 
 
       The registration and licensing of the forklift truck is subject to the following conditions which may be reviewed periodically:
 
  (1)
Driver driving a forklift truck shall possess a valid full driving licence with driving entitlement “Special Purpose Vehicle” (vehicle class 21), and the Transport Department has approved the driver to drive that forklift truck;
 
  (2)
Operation of the forklift truck is restricted to the route as previously approved by the Transport Department;
 
  (3)
The forklift truck is only allowed to be used for carriage of goods;
 
  (4)
Whilst in motion on the road, the fork must be in the lowered position, the driver must ensure that he has a clear field of vision, loads must not obstruct his field of vision;
 
  (5)
Special attention must be paid at all times to the traffic nearby and the safety of pedestrians;
 
  (6)
No undue obstruction should be caused to pedestrians and other vehicles;
 
  (7)
The vehicle licence should be displayed on the forklift truck and driver of the forklift truck must bring along the original of this document when driving, and produce it to police officer for inspection as and when required;
 
  (8)
The vehicle registration mark should be displayed on the back of the vehicle;
 
  (9)
Vehicle owner needs to acquire sufficient and appropriate amount of third party risks insurance for the equipment installed on the forklift truck and operation of such equipment;
 
  (10)
Carriage of passengers is not allowed;
 
  (11)
The maximum vehicle speed shall not exceed 15km/hr;

  (12)
A flashing amber light conforming to the requirements laid down in regulation 111 of the Road Traffic (Construction and Maintenance of Vehicles) Regulations, Cap. 374A, should be displayed on top of the forklift truck at all times when in use on road;
 
  (13)
The forklift truck must be fitted with an automatic device capable of giving audible and sufficient warning when it is reversing and is about to reverse and such device shall conform to the requirements laid down in regulation 38 of the Road Traffic (Construction and Maintenance of Vehicles) Regulations, Cap. 374A as applied to goods vehicles;
 
  (14)
The owner of the forklift truck shall ensure that the forklift truck must be equipped with an efficient service braking system, parking brake system, steering system, adequate lighting system, audible warning system, rear view mirror and other accessories deemed necessary for its safe operation;
 
  (15)
The forklift truck must be properly maintained and in good and safe working conditions for operation at all times.

叉式起重車的發牌條件
 
 
        叉式起重車的登記及發牌須遵照以下條件，而有關條件可予以定期檢討：
 
  (1)
駕駛叉式起重車的司機必須擁有香港正式駕駛執照「特別用途車輛」的駕駛資格(車輛類別代號21)，並已獲運輸署書面批准該司機駕駛該輛叉式起重車；
 
  (2)
叉式起重車只限於已獲運輸署批准的路線上運作；
 
  (3)
叉式起重車只容許用作載運貨物；
 
  (4)
在道路上行走時，鏟叉必須放下，司機須確保其視野清晰，及載荷物不得阻礙其視線；
 
  (5)
在任何時間均須特別注意附近的交通情況及關顧行人安全；
 
  (6)
不得對行人及其他車輛造成不必要的阻礙；
 
  (7)
必須在車輛上展示車輛牌照，而司機在駕駛該車輛時需攜帶本文件(正本)，以便在有需要時供警務人員查閱；
 
  (8)
須在車尾展示車輛登記號碼；
 
  (9)
車主必須為叉式起重車上裝設的設備及其操作購買足夠及合適的第三者保險；
 
  (10)
不得接載乘客；
 
  (11)
最高車速不得超逾每小時15公里；
 
  (12)
當叉式起重車在道路上使用時，須時刻在車頂閃亮琥珀色的燈，該燈須符合《道路交通（車輛構造及保養）規例》（第374A章）第111條所訂下的規定；
 
  (13)
叉式起重車必須裝配一個能夠在其倒車時及即將倒車時發出聲音並可給予充分警告的自動裝置，而有關裝置須符合《道路交通（車輛構造及保養）規例》（第374A章）第38條適用於貨車的規定；

  (14)
車主須確保叉式起重車配備有效的行車制動系統、停泊制動系統、轉向系統、充足的照明系統、發聲警告系統、倒後鏡及安全操作所需的其他附件；
 
  (15)
叉式起重車必須維修妥善，並時刻保持良好和安全操作狀況。
