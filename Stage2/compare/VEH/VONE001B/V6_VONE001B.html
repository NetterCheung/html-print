注意 Notes for Attention

  1. 登記車主須按照第374E章第25條的規定於車輛展示有效的車輛牌照，該車輛方可在路上出現或使用。
     Registered vehicle owners must display a valid vehicle licence on the vehicle in accordance with the requirements stipulated in regulation 25 of Cap. 374E when the vehicle is on or be used on any roads.
  2. 如牌照上顯示的車輛資料沒有改變，車主在往後每次續牌後可沿用同一張不載有屆滿日期的車輛牌照。如車輛資料（如顏色）有所改變，車主須向運輸署遞交《更改個人資料或車輛資料通知書》(TD559)；如車輛牌照已遺失或損毀，車主則須向運輸署遞交《車輛登記文件複本╱車輛牌照複本申請表》(TD151)。申請獲批後，車主將獲發新的車輛牌照或車輛牌照複本，以供在車輛上展示。
     If the particulars on the licence are unchanged, the owner can continue to use the same licence with no printed expiry date after each subsequent renewal. If any vehicle particular (e.g. colour) is changed, the owner must submit a "Notice of Change of Personal Particulars or Vehicle Particulars" (TD559) to the Transport Department (TD). If the licence is lost or defaced, the owner must submit an "Application for a Duplicate Vehicle Registration Document/ Duplicate Vehicle Licence" (TD151) to the TD. After the application is approved, the owner will be issued with a new vehicle licence or a duplicate vehicle licence for display on the vehicle.
  3. 你可以掃瞄右列二維碼，透過運輸署的車輛牌照網上查詢平台，即時查閱名下車輛的牌照屆滿日期。
     You may scan the QR code on the right and access the Transport Department's Vehicle Licence Online Enquiry Platform to instantly check the vehicle licence expiry date of your vehicle.
