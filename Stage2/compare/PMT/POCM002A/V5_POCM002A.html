Conditions of Permit

 

 

1 .

使用行駛許可證的車輛在路上行駛時，必須遵守香港法例第374章道路交通 ( 車輛登記及領牌 ) 規例第53條的法例規定及許可證的條件。

 

When a vehicle to which a movement permit relates is being moved on a road it must comply with the statutory requirements of Regulations 53 of the Road Traffic (Registration and Licensing of Vehicles) Regulations, Cap. 374 and the conditions of permit.

 

 

2 .

行駛許可證只授權有關的車輛在許可證的12個月有效期內最多在路上行駛12天，如須行駛更多次數，則必須領取一個新的許可證。車輛只可以於許可證所指明的日期行駛。

 

The movement permit only authorises the vehicle to be driven on a road for a maximum of 12 days during the 12-month validity period of the permit. A fresh permit should be obtained if more trips are required. Movement of the vehicle is restricted only to the dates as specified in the permit.

 

 

3 .

該車 ( 拖車除外 ) 在道路行駛時，必須依照汽車保險 ( 第三者風險 ) 條例之規定，預定投保，一如依照道路交通條例及規例的規定作登記及領牌的車輛一樣。

 

When the vehicle (with the exception of a trailer) is driven on a road, it shall be covered by such insurance required by the Motor Vehicle Insurance (Third Party Risks) Ordinance as if it were a motor vehicle registered and licensed in accordance with the provisions of the Road Traffic Ordinance and the regulations made thereunder.

 

 

4 .

車輛的司機必須是該類別車輛的有效駕駛執照持有人。

 

The driver of the vehicle must be a holder of a valid driving licence for that class of vehicle.

 

 

5 .

車輛必須裝載緊急警告標誌，於車輛故障時使用；而遇有故障情況，應立即通知警方。

 

Emergency warning signs must be carried and used in the event of a breakdown and the Police should be immediately informed of the situation.

 

 

6 .

當在無亭收費區域內使用該車輛時，許可證持有人／司機各自均須確保:-
(i) 於該車輛的最前部分，垂直展示一塊老爺車行駛許可證字牌，令該字牌上的每個英文字母及數目字均呈垂直，且可從該車輛前方識別；及
(ii) 於該車輛的最後部分，垂直展示一塊老爺車行駛許可證字牌，令該字牌上的每個英文字母及數目字均呈垂直，且可從該車輛後方識別。
(iii)除許可證條件另有規定外，許可證持有人／該車輛的駕駛人各自均須按照《道路交通(車輛登記及領牌)規例》(第374E章) (《規例》)附表4展示老爺車行駛許可證字牌，包括但不限於：
- 以單行或雙行(英文字母在上行而數目字在下行)及按規定的式樣及大小展示老爺車行駛許可證的英文字母及數目字；以及
- 老爺車行駛許可證號碼須按附表4第2段有關登記號碼的展示、顏色、構造、裝配及照明的規定展示在一個反光的字牌上。

 

If the vehicle is being used in any area where boothless tolling applies, the permit holder/driver of the vehicle must each ensure that:-
(i) a movement permit for classic car plate is displayed on the foremost part of the vehicle in a vertical position, so that every letter and numeral of the movement permit for classic car plate is vertical and is distinguishable from the front of the vehicle;
(ii) a movement permit for classic car plate is displayed on the rearmost part of the vehicle in a vertical position, so that every letter and numeral of the movement permit for classic car plate is vertical and is distinguishable from the rear of the vehicle; and
(iii)Except as prescribed in the permit conditions, the movement permit for classic car plate is displayed in accordance with Schedule 4 of Road Traffic (Registration and Licensing of Vehicles) Regulations (Cap. 374E), including but not limited to:-
- display the letters and numerals of the movement permit number for classic car in a single row or in 2 rows, with the letters in the upper row and the numerals in the lower row and in the required form and size; and
- movement permit number for classic car shall be displayed on a reflex-reflecting number plate in accordance with the requirements on the display, colours, construction, fitting and illumination of registration marks stated in paragraph 2 of Schedule 4.

 

 

7 .

許可證必須展示在車頭擋風玻璃附近使別人能在車輛前面清楚看到。

 

The permit must be displayed near the front windscreen of the vehicle in such a manner that it is clearly visible from the front of the vehicle.

 

 

8 .

車輛在路上行駛時，除了通常載於車上的設備，備件或燃油外，不得裝載其他物品。

 

When the vehicle is being moved on a road, no load is permitted on the vehicle other than such equipment, spares or fuel as normally carried on the vehicle.

 

 

9 .

如違反許可證的條件，許可證可能遭取消，而車主則可能被罰款二千元。

 

Contravention of any permit conditions may render the permit liable to cancellation and the vehicle owner liable to a fine of $2,000.

 

 

10 .

許可證如遭註銷，必須退還運輸署署長。

 

The permit must be returned to the Commissioner for Transport in case of cancellation.
