Conditions of Permit

 

Chassis No. LV4520P447
252 		Date of issue 09/07/2026 Transaction A01-0017 No . MM2500300

1.

該車只准由該類車輛的有效駕駛執照的持有人在路上駕駛。

 

The vehicle is to be driven on the road only by a person holding a valid driving licence authorizing him to drive a vehicle of that class.

2.

許可證持有人在該車輛進入有管理機關或擁有人限制車輛進出的任何地方或私家路，必須取得管理機關或擁有人預先批准。

 

The permit holder shall seek the approval from the relevant authority prior to driving the vehicle in any area or private road of which the management authority or owner may restrict the access of the vehicle.

3.

如有違反任何發證條件，許可證可能會被取消。

 

Contravention of any of the permit conditions may render the permit liable to cancellation.

4.

該車 ( 拖車除外 ) 在道路行駛時，必須依照汽車保險 ( 第三者風險 ) 條例之規定，預定投保，一如依照道路交通條例及規例的規定作登記及領牌的車輛一樣。

 

When the vehicle (with the exception of a trailer) is driven on a road, it shall be covered by such insurance required by the Motor Vehicle Insurance (Third Party Risks) Ordinance as if it were a motor vehicle registered and licensed in accordance with the provisions of the Road Traffic Ordinance and the regulations made thereunder.

5.

當該車在道路行駛時，除該車輛通常運載的裝備、零件或燃料外，不得運載其他負載物。

 

When the vehicle is being driven on a road, no load is carried on the vehicle other than such equipment, spares or fuel normally carried on the vehicle.

6.

該車在道路行駛時，應準備一輛可以隨時出動的搶救車輛。

 

A recovery vehicle should be available all the time the vehicle is on the road.

7.

該車必須備有緊急警告標誌，在發生故障時一定要加以使用。

 

Emergency warning signs must be carried and used in the event of a breakdown.

8.

該車發生故障時，必須通知警方。

 

Police should be informed when a breakdown occurs.

9.

車上必須展示一個24小時適用的電話號碼，以便警方與該車車主或其代表聯絡。

 

A telephone number is to be displayed on the vehicle which will provide Police with 24 hours access to the owner or his representative.

10.

此許可證必須以從車輛前面能清楚看見的方式，展示在車輛左手邊的顯眼處。

 

The permit shall be displayed in a conspicuous place on the left-hand side of the vehicle in such a manner that it is clearly visible from the front of the vehicle.

11.

許可證持有人須遵守運輸署署長於任何時候指明的其他條件。

 

The permit holder shall comply such other conditions as may be specified by the Commissioner for Transport from time to time.

12.

車輛只限於球會維修及保安之用。

 

The vehicles are limited to Club maintenance and security vehicles.

13.

車輛只可橫過粉錦公路到達球會主要入口。

 

The vehicles only cross Fan Kam Road at the main entrance to the Club.

14.

有關車輛除在球會正門入口外交通燈控制的過路處橫過粉錦公路外，不得在任何其他道路上行駛。

 

The vehicles must not be driven on any road, except crossing Fan Kam Road at the traffic light controlled crossing situation at the main entrance to the Club.

15.

車輛於天黑時間橫過馬路時必須有適當照明。

 

The vehicles should be properly lit during hours of darkness when using the crossing.

16.

車輛必須配備警報儀器。

 

The vehicles are to be fitted with audible warning device.

17.

許可證在取消後，必須歸還運輸署署長。

 

The permit must be returned to the Commissioner for Transport in case of cancellation.

18.

車輛(包括所有車身及配件在內)必須妥為保養，時刻維持良好及可使用的操作狀態。

 

The vehicle including all body work and fittings must be properly maintained in good and serviceable conditions for operation at all time.

19.

車輛駕駛人 / 營辦者應遵照上述車輛的營運者手冊載述的指示辦理，以策安全。

 

The driver of the vehicle / operator shall observe the instructions in the operator´s manual of the captioned vehicle as to ensure safety vehicle usage.

20.

此類車輛只限持有有效駕駛執照以駕駛特別用途車輛的人士在路上駕駛。

 

The vehicle shall be driven on a road only by a person holding a valid driving licence authorizing him to drive special purpose vehicles.

21.

許可證持有人／車輛駕駛人必須確保不得超過有關車輛的最高重量。

 

The owner/driver of the vehicle should ensure the maximum weight of vehicle is not exceeded.

22.

許可證持有人／車輛駕駛人須確保車輛配備有效的行車制動系統、停放制動系統、轉向系統、充足的照明系統、發聲警報系統和車輛安全運作所需的其他配件。

 

The permit holder / driver of the vehicle should ensure that the vehicle must be equipped with an efficient service braking system, parking brake system, steering system, adequate lighting system, audible warning system and other accessories deemed necessary for its safe operation.
