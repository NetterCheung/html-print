3. 運載特長貨物許可證在運輸署署長指明的期間內有效。
  A long load permit shall be valid for the period specified by the Commissioner for Transport.
4. 許可證須在車輛前擋風玻璃靠近車輛牌照處展示。
  This permit shall be displayed on the front windscreen of the vehicle near to the vehicle licence.
5. 任何負載物必須適當地固定在該車輛上，而負載物並無任何部份接觸道路及無任何負載物 ( 包括懸出的負載物 ) 是全部或部份由該車輛的後擋板承托或支撐。
  Any load shall be properly secured to the vehicle, and no portion of it shall touch the road. No load (including an overhanging load) shall be, in whole or in part, carried on, or supported by, the tailgate of the vehicle.
6. 如在並非黑夜時間或在並非屬能見度低的情況下載有負載物，在該車輛的負載物的後端必須展示一塊面積不少於一平方米的紅旗。
  If the load is carried other than in the hours of darkness or conditions of poor visibility, a red flag of an area not less than one square metre shall be displayed at the rear extremity of the load.
7. 在黑夜時間及在能見度低的情況下，如果該車輛的負載物懸出車前，負載物的前端每一邊均展示有一盞朝前方照射的白燈；及負載物的後端展示一盞朝後方照射的紅燈。
  During the hours of darkness and in conditions of poor visibility, a white light showing ahead shall be affixed at each side of the front extremity of the load in situations where such a load overhangs the front of the vehicle, and a red light shall show to the rear at the rear extremity of the load.
8. 此許可證亦須受警務處處長建議的其他條件限制。
  The permit shall be subject to additional conditions recommended by the Commissioner of Police.
9. 如違反許可證上任何條件，許可證會予取消；被取消之許可證，須交還運輸署署長。
  Contravention of any permit conditions may render the permit liable to cancellation in which case it shall be returned to the Commissioner for Transport.
10. 任何司機不得在道路上駕駛載有以下負載物的車輛﹕負載物的高度超過4.6米，或雖低於該高度，但卻可能導致在該道路上空合法架設的任何物體或電綫損壞。
  No driver shall drive on a road a vehicle that is so loaded that the load rises to such a height in excess of 4.6 metres or to such lesser height as may cause damage to any object or wires lawfully erected above the road.
11. 必須遵守申請書上所列明的運送路綫/範圍；但警方的總區交通部可更改有關的運送路綫/範圍。
  The route / area of transportation on the application is to be observed. However it is subject to variation by Regional Traffic Police.
12. 在每次運送有關負載物之前，必須通知警方的總區交通部。警方保留護送該車輛運送有關負載物，並為提供警方服務收取適當費用的權利。

13. 必須遵從任何穿著制服警務人員的指示。
  The directions of any police officer in uniform are to be obeyed.
14. 長度超逾15米而不能分開的負載物，通常須以設有適當尺寸的載貨平台的掛接車輛運送。
  Indivisible loads exceeding 15 metres in length should normally be carried on an articulated vehicle with a load platform of appropriate size.
15. 如負載物的寬度達3.5米或以上，必須根據「車輛載貨守則」以護送車陪同運送。
  Loads of 3.5 metres or more in width must be accompanied by escort vehicles in accordance with "The Code of Practice for Loading Vehicles".
16. 如未事先安排警方監督，不得在黑夜時間運送寬度達3.5米或以上的負載物。
  Movements of loads of 3.5 metres or more in width during the hours of darkness are not permitted without pre-arranged police supervision.
17. 根據《行車隧道(政府)規例》(第368A章)第14條，申請人須就該車輛申領許可證方可通過隧道。許可證的申請須於擬通過隧道前最少48小時向有關的隧道營辦商作出。
  According to section 14 of Road Tunnels (Government) Regulations, Cap.368A, a permit should be obtained for the passage of that vehicle. Application for the permit shall be made to the respective tunnel operators at least 48 hours before the intended passage.
18. 如本許可證所指的車輛未購買有效保險，本許可證將告無效。
  The permit will be voided if the vehicle(s) referred to is/are not validly insured.
19. 所駕駛的車輛及拖車 ( 如有 ) 須領有牌照，本許可證方可有效使用。
  This permit shall be valid for use when the vehicle and the trailer (if any) to be driven is/are licensed.
20. 許可證持有人在任何地區或私家路運載特長及/或特闊貨物前，必須就進入該等可能被管理當局或擁有人限制該車輛進入的地區/私家路，申請有關當局的批准。
  The permit holder shall seek the approval from the relevant authority prior to carrying long load and/ or wide load in any area or private road of which the management authority or owner may restrict the access of the vehicle.
21. 許可證持有人必須預先檢視行車路線（例如進行視察），確保有足夠空間讓車輛通過及上落貨物。道路安全與其他車輛通行不應受影響。許可證持有人亦須遵守行車路線上任何現有的車輛闊度、長度、高度及重量限制。如許可證持有人對行車路線上的空間限制或道路安全有疑問，須向合資格人士尋求專業意見。
  The permit holder shall review the route in advance (such as pre-inspection) to ensure there are sufficient spaces for passage and loading / unloading of the vehicle. Road safety and other traffic movement shall not be affected. The permit holder shall also observe any prevailing width, length, height and weight restrictions on the route. In the event of doubt about the spatial constraint or road safety along the route, the permit holder shall seek professional advice from a competent person.
22. 如負載物外伸 / 合併負載物外伸超出《道路交通（交通管制）規例》（第374G章）第55條所容許的限度，必須根據「車輛載貨守則」提供護送車輛，並必須就護送車輛的類型和數目事先取得警方同意。護送車輛須根據《道路交通（車輛構造及保養）規例》（第374A章）第111條的規定裝上琥珀色閃動燈號，及須展示在顯眼的位置，即在車輛的前面，後面或車頂上如申請書上圖A或B的適當標誌（ 惟不得遮蔽琥珀色閃動燈號）。如屬前面護送車輛，該標誌必須在車輛前面展示，使之面對迎面而來的車輛；如屬後面護送車輛，該標誌必須在車輛後面展示，使之面對尾隨車輛；如裝在護送車輛的車頂上，則該標誌可為雙面。
  Escort Vehicles must be provided in accordance with "The Code of Practice of Loading of Vehicles if the overhanging / combined overhanging exceeds the limits of Reg. 55, Road Traffic (Traffic Control) Regulations (Cap. 374G). Agreement to the police concerning the types and number of escort vehicles must be sought in advance. An escort vehicle shall be equipped with an amber flashing light in accordance with Reg. 111 of the Road Traffic (Construction and Maintenance of Vehicle) Regulations (Cap. 374A), and shall display in a prominent position, either at the front, rear or on the roof of the vehicle (but such that the flashing light is not obscured), a sign conforming with diagram "A" or "B" of the application form as appropriate. On the leading escort vehicle the sign shall be displayed to the front so as to face oncoming vehicles, and on the trailing escort vehicle the sign shall be displayed to the rear to face following vehicles. When mounted on the roof of an escort vehicle, signs may be double-sided.
